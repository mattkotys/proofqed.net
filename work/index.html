Incoming inspection

In this fictional workflow, receiving staff check a delivery and record the result. Quality reviews any discrepancy before the delivery enters stock.

Work instruction

Before you start: Have the purchase order, packing slip, and applicable inspection criteria available.

  1. Match the part number and quantity against the purchase order. Record the lot identifier when applicable.
  2. Check the delivery against the inspection criteria. Record the result and any discrepancy.
  3. If the checks pass, record the release and move the delivery to stock. If a check fails or a criterion is missing, hold the delivery and notify Quality.

Completion check: The inspection record identifies the delivery, who checked it, and where it went.
